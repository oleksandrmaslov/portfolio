# Publication safety

Before putting field material on the public site:

- blur faces where identity is unnecessary;
- remove EXIF/location metadata from final web exports;
- avoid current deployment locations;
- avoid vehicle identifiers, maps, radio screens, serial numbers and other unnecessary operational details;
- confirm that each field image/video is approved for public publication;
- avoid graphic medical footage;
- do not use the footage to make causal claims such as “this device saved a life” unless that claim is specifically supportable.

The strongest use of the media is to establish an engineering constraint.

Example:

**Good**
> When the vehicle lost its normal lighting, portable Energy Cells were used as independent work lights during evacuation.

**Avoid**
> Our device saved the casualty.
